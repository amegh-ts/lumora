## Lumora 0.14.0 — clearer, steadier, friendlier

### Playing
- **Resume with a choice:** when a video picks up where you left off, a notice says "Resumed at 42:10" with a **Start Over** button.
- **Up next:** when a video ends and another is queued, a card shows the next title with a 5-second countdown, plus **Play Now** and **Cancel**.
- **Skip feedback:** jumping back or forward shows « 10 / 10 » on that side of the video. Quick repeats add up ("30 s »").
- **Picture in Picture button** on the control bar, and every control-bar button's tooltip now shows its keyboard shortcut.
- **Volume boost** is easy to spot: the volume slider turns orange above 100%.
- **Screenshots** flash briefly and offer **Show in Finder**, instead of opening Finder every time.

### Easier to use
- **Go to Time** (⌘J) is a dark panel with a slider kept in step with the time you type, and it shows the current position and length.
- **Open URL** (⌘U) is dark too, and has a **Recent Links** menu.
- **Continue Watching** on the empty screen shows cards with a picture from where you stopped, the time left and a progress bar. Online videos are included now too.
- **Error messages** on the empty screen appear in a clear card.
- **Make Lumora your video player:** a one-time offer on first launch, and a button in Settings › General (macOS asks you to confirm).
- **Empty-screen hint line:** ⌘O Open · ⌘U Link · ⇧⌘T Live TV · ⌘/ Shortcuts.

### Playlist
- Each item shows its number, length and folder; the item that's playing is highlighted.
- **Drag to reorder.** Delete removes and Return plays the selected item.
- **Save Playlist** as an `.m3u` file. The header shows the number of items and the total length.

### Converting
- **Real progress:** "42% · 3 min left · 2.1× speed" with a filling bar.
- **Background finish:** the Dock icon bounces when a conversion finishes while you're in another app.

### Stability
- **Picture in Picture safety:** its controls are now safe whichever thread macOS uses to call them, the same class of problem as the crash fixed in 0.13.1.

### Fixed
- **Live TV in Continue Watching:** watching a channel no longer adds it to your Library (channels have no point to resume).
- **Continue Watching** could be empty even when there were videos to resume.
- **Settings:** the subtitle colour uses a compact colour swatch.

### Install / update
Existing installs are offered this update automatically (Lumora → Check for Updates…).
New installs: download `Lumora-0.14.0.dmg` and drag Lumora into Applications. First launch only: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (not notarized yet).
