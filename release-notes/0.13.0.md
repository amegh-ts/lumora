## Lumora 0.13.0 — Live TV, made for watching

Live TV now lives inside the player, built around what you do most on TV: changing the channel.

### Live TV redesign
- **No separate window.** The Live TV panel slides in over the video (⇧⌘T, the TV button on the control bar, or Live TV on the empty screen). Pasting a channel-list link into Open URL keeps you in the player and opens the panel.
- **Easy browsing:**
  - The All tab starts with tiles: All Channels, your own country, then categories from biggest to smallest, then the countries with the most channels (with flags).
  - Inside a list, category and country filter chips narrow it down (e.g. News + India). Long lists, like countries, can be searched.
  - You can also search by channel name or number.
- **Favourites and Recent tabs.** The panel opens on Favourites if you have any, otherwise on Recent.
- **Change channels like a TV:**
  - Channel up/down buttons replace Previous/Next on the control bar.
  - ⌘↑ / ⌘↓, Page Up / Page Down, N and the media keys also change channel.
  - Type a channel number to tune, and ⌫ goes back to the previous channel.
  - Channel up/down stays within what you were browsing, for example your favourites, or News in India.
- **Channel banner:** each switch shows the logo, number, name, category, country, LIVE and a star. Press I to show it again. ⌘D adds the channel to Favourites, or click a star anywhere.
- **Channel numbers** stay the same when the list updates.
- **Dead channels:**
  - Pick one directly and Lumora says so and stays on it.
  - Reach one with channel up/down and it counts down 3 seconds (with a Stay Here button), then moves on.
  - Channels that failed are dimmed for the rest of the session.
- **Watching TV no longer replaces your playlist of files,** and the playlist button hides while a channel plays.
- **Continue watching:** the empty screen offers to continue the last channel you watched.

### Lighter
- **Nothing about Live TV loads until you use it.** Idle memory at launch dropped from 62 MB to 36 MB.
- **Released when you're done:** a minute after you close the panel (with no channel playing), the channel lists and logos are freed again.

### Other improvements
- Long choice lists (fonts in Settings, countries and categories in Live TV) no longer fill the whole screen. They open at a fixed height and can be searched.
- Crash reports only include player messages from just before the crash.
- Favourites and recent channels from 0.12.0 carry over.

### Install / update
Existing installs are offered this update automatically (Lumora → Check for Updates…).
New installs: download `Lumora-0.13.0.dmg` and drag Lumora into Applications. First launch only: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (not notarized yet).
