## Lumora 0.9.0 — redesigned windows

### Redesigned
- **Keyboard Shortcuts** (Help › Keyboard Shortcuts, ⌘/): a proper window instead of a text box. Shortcuts are grouped, keys look like keys, and you can search.
- **Library** (⇧⌘L):
  - Filters: All, In Progress and Watched, plus search.
  - Each item shows a progress bar and when you last played it.
  - Right-click to play, show in Finder, copy the link, mark as watched or unwatched, or remove. Return plays the selection and Delete removes it.
  - Clear History asks before forgetting everything.
- **Subtitle search**:
  - Choose the language; your languages from Settings come first.
  - Results are sorted by popularity, and subtitles for the deaf and hard of hearing are marked SDH.
  - One click on "Add to Video" adds the subtitle to what's playing.
  - Clear messages when the API key is missing or rejected, when OpenSubtitles is busy, or when you're offline.
- **Convert Media**:
  - Drop a file onto the window, or click to choose one.
  - Each format has a short description of where it plays.
  - Choose where to save, cancel while converting, then show the result in Finder.
- **Open URL** fills in a link that's already on your clipboard, and accepts links typed without https://.
- **About Lumora** has a short description and links to the website, issue reporting and licenses.

### Fixes
- The Library window could open as a 1-pixel-wide strip and seem not to open at all.
- YouTube and other online videos now show their real title instead of the link, in the window, playlist, Now Playing and Library.

### Install / update
Existing installs are offered this update automatically (Lumora → Check for Updates…).
New installs: download `Lumora-0.9.0.dmg` and drag Lumora into Applications. First launch only: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (not notarized yet).
