## Lumora 0.12.0 — Live TV

### New
- **Channels** (Window › Channels, ⇧⌘T): watch live TV from channel lists (M3U), the way VLC opens them, with a proper browser for them.
  - **Add a list in one click:** iptv-org's 11,000+ free, legal channels from around the world, or its lists by country, news, movies, music and sports. You can also paste the M3U link from your TV provider or choose a file (File › Add Channel List…).
  - **Pasting a channel-list link into Open URL** now adds every channel to Channels instead of playing only the first one. Single stream links still play straight away.
  - **Find channels** by category (with counts), country or search, with each channel's logo.
  - **Favourites and Recently Watched**; right-click a channel to add it or copy its link.
  - **Switch channels** with ⌘← / ⌘→ while watching. Channels show the LIVE badge.
  - **Lists stay on your Mac** and refresh once a day; you can update, rename or remove them.
  - **Channels that need a particular browser identity or referring site** (`#EXTVLCOPT` in the list) now play.
- **When a channel is offline,** Lumora says so and stays on it, instead of skipping ahead. Free channels often go offline or are blocked outside their country.

### Security
- Channel lists downloaded from the internet can only point at internet streams, not at files on your Mac.
- List and logo downloads stop at a size limit (40 MB and 512 KB) as they arrive.

### Coming next
TV guide (what's on now and next), IPTV service logins and internet radio. See #45.

### Install / update
Existing installs are offered this update automatically (Lumora → Check for Updates…).
New installs: download `Lumora-0.12.0.dmg` and drag Lumora into Applications. First launch only: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (not notarized yet).
